Dressed up, as always

Some people make a room brighter just by walking into it. You do it in a kitchen at night, on a bus with a suitcase, in front of a banner that says Congratulations, and in a sparkly kurta under fairy lights.

You work hard, you laugh loudly, you know every word of the songs that matter, and you keep chasing the music even when the schedule says otherwise. I love watching that.

Thank you for being the kind of friend who makes ordinary days into good stories. I hope this year is full of stages, standing ovations, and cake that is actually the size you wanted.
